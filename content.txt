5. CLINICAL HERITAGE & HISTORICAL MILESTONES (13+ YEARS OF EXCELLENCE)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
* 2013 - Foundation of Child Learning Centre:
  - CRC launched the Child Learning Centre specifically to address academic skill development.
  - Context: Many school-going children with good cognitive abilities faced hidden challenges in reading, writing, mathematical calculations, and social skills.
  - The centre was established to deliver personalized, ability-based interventions to help children overcome academic hurdles and achieve life-long confidence.

* 2014 - Psychological Counselling & Environmental Assessments:
  - Psychological counselling integrated into core clinical services to support families by addressing emotional and psychological needs.
  - Launched Home and School site assessments to ensure children receive consistent therapeutic support across all their real-world environments.

* 2015 - Father-Child Bonding & Parental Play Programs:
  - Launched a specialized "Father-Child Bonding" series to highlight and empower the father's active role in child neurodevelopment.
  - Introduced "Play and Communication" programs empowering parents with evidence-based developmental techniques to practice at home.

* 2016 - Parenting Workshops & Remedial Education:
  - Initiated Home Program Parenting Workshops teaching strategies for speech, communication, and emotional regulation across all childhood stages.
  - Introduced structured Remedial Education sessions to help children with learning challenges excel in school examinations.

* November 2017 - LD Rehab Centre (Sion):
  - Established at D.S. High School in Sion, inaugurated by Dr. Rukhshana Shonapurwala.
  - Dedicated to providing rehabilitation and academic support to underprivileged school-going children.

* March 2018 - Vashi Centre Launch:
  - Inaugurated by MLA Shri Sandeepji Naik and Dr. Shashi Oberai (Vice President of AIOTA).
  - Expanded child development services across Navi Mumbai.

* September 2018 - Nerul Facility Expansion:
  - Nerul centre relocated to a significantly larger, modern clinical facility to accommodate growing demand and multi-disciplinary therapies.

* May 2019 - Nagpur Regional Centre:
  - CRC expanded 800 km away from Mumbai headquarters into Nagpur.
  - Successfully introduced the ability-based therapeutic model, catering to over 60+ families.

* Subsequent Expansions:
  - Established dedicated centres in Thane, Chembur, Ulwe, Nashik, and Dombivali.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Q1: What kind of educational and therapeutic services does Child Learning Center offer?
A1: Child Learning Center offers comprehensive tailored services including intellectual skill enhancement, remedial education, tutoring, test preparation, behavioral therapy, occupational therapy, and academic coaching.

Q2: How are Child Learning Center's tutors and therapists selected?
A2: All tutors, therapists, and clinicians undergo a rigorous selection process that includes thorough background checks, subject matter and clinical expertise evaluations, and multiple clinical interview rounds.

Q3: What is the pricing for Child Learning Center's services?
