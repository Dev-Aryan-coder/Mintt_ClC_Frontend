The Philosophy

Why I built this approach

“You are not a bad parent. Your child is not a bad kid. You are both good people having a hard time.”

Traditional parenting focuses entirely on behavior modification — rewards, timeouts, and punishments. Good Inside flips this script by looking at the emotion *underneath* the behavior.

When parents learn to hold boundaries firmly without shaming or disconnecting, kids naturally build emotional regulation that lasts for life.
