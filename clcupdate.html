About CLC

About Child Learning Centre

Child Learning Centre (CLC) is dedicated to supporting children through personalized learning, developmental therapies, and compassionate care.

Established in 2013 under CRC, CLC brings together specialists in speech and language therapy, occupational therapy, special education, and child counseling. We work closely with parents and schools to understand each child's needs, build essential skills, and support meaningful progress.

Clinical & Educational Services

Specialized Therapies for Real Progress

View All Programs

Speech & Language Therapy

Overcoming articulation barriers, stuttering, language delays, and communicative anxiety with engaging, interactive techniques.

Occupational Therapy

Enhancing fine and gross motor coordination, sensory balance, attention regulation, and everyday functional independence.

Remedial & Special Education

Targeted reading, writing, spelling, and dyscalculia interventions formulated around individual learning pace and school curricula.

Child & Adolescent Counseling

Constructive emotional coping mechanisms for anxiety, behavioral challenges, peer relations, and school transition pressures.

Learning Disability (LD) Rehab

Certified clinical remediation for Dyslexia, Dysgraphia, and Dyscalculia with school-board certified accommodations.
